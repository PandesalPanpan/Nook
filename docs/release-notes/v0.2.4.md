# Nook 0.2.4

## Clarify your Inbox

- Process one captured thought at a time. Edit its wording, turn it into a Task or Note, or split it into a linked Task and Note.
- Add a Task or Note to a Project, Area, or Resource, and connect related items without merging them.
- Schedule Tasks with quick dates or the in-app calendar. Deadlines remain separate and optional.
- Reopen processed thoughts from History with their original wording, capture time, and links to the results.
- Keep Archive reversible and require confirmation before deleting a thought.

## Reliability and compatibility

- Clarification drafts and processing continue to work offline. Repeated processing reuses the same result records and preserves capture history and attachments.
- V1 records remain readable; new V2 fields support processed history, Resource membership, source links, and related items.
- Android Inbox capture times show relative ages for recent thoughts and a local date and time for older thoughts.
